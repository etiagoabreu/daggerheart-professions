# Professions

New Homebrew Cards for Professions, created using the Daggerheart Homebrew Kit and following the DPCGL license.


In addition to their adventuring abilities, each character may possess a Profession, representing the trade, training, or occupation they practiced before or during their life as an adventurer.

Professions are intended to work alongside Daggerheart's philosophy of collaborative storytelling and worldbuilding with the GM, encouraging players to actively contribute to the setting through their characters' expertise and experiences.

Each Profession grants the character:

- Two unique Abilities, determined by the chosen profession.
- Professional Narrative Authority, a special rule that allows the character to temporarily take over the narration of certain scenes.

# Professional Narrative Authority

Whenever a scene directly involves knowledge, activities, or situations related to a character's Profession, that player may declare that they wish to take over the narration of the scene.

While the scene lasts, the player may describe details of the environment, introduce minor NPCs, establish common knowledge related to their trade, and narrate the outcomes of actions connected to their area of expertise.

The GM remains responsible for:

- Portraying important NPCs;
- Determining mechanical consequences;
- Calling for rolls when necessary;
- Vetoing elements that conflict with the established fiction or disrupt the adventure.

Control of the scene returns to the GM as normal once the focus is no longer related to the Profession or when the scene ends.

# Limitations

Professional Narrative Authority exists to enrich the story and collaboratively expand the setting. It cannot be used to:

- Create rare, magical, or legendary items without the GM's approval;
- Gain mechanical advantages not granted by the Profession's rules;
- Introduce NPCs important to the main storyline;
- Alter facts already established in the narrative;
- Avoid significant consequences, challenges, or conflicts in the adventure;
- Automatically determine the success of actions or negotiations.

Whenever there is uncertainty regarding the limits of Professional Narrative Authority, the GM has the final say.

# Examples
A Blacksmith may lead a scene involving a forge, appraising weapons, or negotiating with artisans.
A Cook may take over a scene while preparing an important meal or searching for rare ingredients.
A Merchant may lead trade negotiations, auctions, or interactions with caravans.
An Alchemist may lead scenes related to gathering reagents, working in laboratories, or identifying unusual substances.
